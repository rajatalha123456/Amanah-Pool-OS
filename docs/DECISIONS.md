# Decision record

Where the BRD is silent, a decision was needed. Each one below was taken by the development side using its best judgement,
with the reason, how to change it, and **who still has to confirm it**. Items marked **Professional sign-off** concern tax,
Shariah or legal matters that no software team can settle: the platform is configured so that the confirmed value is a
setting, not a code change.

| # | Decision | Reason | How to change it | Confirm with |
|---|---|---|---|---|

A payout batch is refused if the pack has no rates. | The BRD names no rate; a rate hard-coded in code would be wrong the day the law changes. | Edit the rule pack (Administration > Jurisdiction & Rule Packs). | **Professional sign-off**: a tax adviser must confirm the rates and the filer / non-filer basis before real use. |
| 2 | **Zakat is not deducted at source** by default. ## What these decisions do not replace
Nothing here is a fatwa, a tax opinion or legal advice (the BRD says the same of itself). Items 1, 2, 3 and 7 must be confirmed
by the named professionals before the platform handles real customer money.

| 13 | **A reserve policy's balance is system-controlled** (it moves only with signed runs and reversals); the rate and cap, and creating a policy, are proposals approved by a different **finance checker**. | BRD 5 (reserves need approval) and 11 (overrides need maker and checker).
